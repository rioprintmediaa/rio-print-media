⬆ Upscale Image
Honest limitation: this is high-quality resizing plus sharpening, done entirely in your browser — not AI-based super-resolution. It won't invent realistic new detail the way tools like Photoshop's Super Resolution or Topaz do. It genuinely helps for modest upscales, but a very low-res image blown up 4x will still look soft, just less blocky than a plain resize.
